\section*{From Raw Scores to Percentage Importance}
While the raw feature importance scores (calculated by summing MSE reductions) are useful for ranking features, they are often normalized to be expressed as percentages.  
This makes them easier to interpret as the relative contribution of each feature to the overall model performance.

\[
\text{Percentage Importance}(j) = 
\left( \frac{\mathrm{Imp}(j)}{\sum_{k=1}^{N} \mathrm{Imp}(k)} \right) \times 100\%
\]
